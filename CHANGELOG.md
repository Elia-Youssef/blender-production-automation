## [0.1.0] - 2026-07-13

### Added

- Metadata-driven PDF, customer, bottle, and asset-routing workflow.
- Region-aware customer matching and box/pouch die-code detection.
- Configurable consolidated Blender asset-library lookup.
- Safe image-renaming and folder-size utilities.
- Photoshop batch-processing integration for transparent PNG and JPG output.
- Public example configuration with private local overrides.
- Automated tests, formatting, linting, and cross-platform CI.
